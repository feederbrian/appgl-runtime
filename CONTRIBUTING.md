# Contributing

**Contributions are not being accepted yet.**

A substantial internal rework is planned, and it will change enough of the codebase that work done
against the current tree would not survive it. Rather than accept patches and then invalidate them,
this project is holding contributions closed until that lands.

## What this means right now

- **Pull requests will not be reviewed or merged.**
- **Issues are not being triaged.** Bug reports are welcome to sit, but expect no response.
- **Security findings are the exception** — see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and report them
  privately rather than publicly.

## What will change

This document will be replaced with real guidance — build instructions, test expectations, coding
conventions, and a review process — once the rework is complete and the tree is stable enough for
outside work to hold its value.

Thanks for your interest. Please check back.
